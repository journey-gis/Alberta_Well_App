Alberta Well Development

Selected Fields Over the Last Decade (2016-present)

Resource extraction within Alberta is an integral part of the province making up approximately 25% of the province's gross domestic product. In order to produce oil & gas within Alberta the producer must lease the mineral rights from the owner of those rights, this is normally the Government of Alberta. When oil & gas is produced from on of these wells, royalties are paid to the government. Oil & Gas production within Alberta is not just of interest to the companies the produce it, but to the people who live within the province.

Oil and Gas within the province of Alberta is regulated by the Alberta Energy Regulator. Directive 51 of the government details how information from the drilling and production of wells is reported to the government, because of this Alberta has a large well database that is available to producers and in many cases the general public. Data companies exist, which package this data and serve it to the industry within custom mapping packages or database connections, however the general public does not usually have such access. There are online mapping portals provided by the government, but for the vast majority of people they are complicated and comprise large amounts of data.

This project aims to create a simple, intuitive, picture of the development of oil and gas wells within the province. The current iteration of this project is not designed to be a definitive representation of wells within the province, but instead show a few selected fields and what development looked like in that field. Development in terms of this dashboard is solely realized through the drilling of new wells.
